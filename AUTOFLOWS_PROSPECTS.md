## Outreach Note — Title Targeting Adjustment

For companies under 200 employees, a dedicated COO or VP Ops often does not exist. Target the CEO or co-founder directly — they own ops at this stage and have the highest decision-making authority.

For companies 200–500 employees: target VP Operations, VP Revenue Operations, or COO. If none exists publicly, target CEO.

## DM Template (LinkedIn — no email)

> Hey [Name], noticed [Company] has been scaling fast. At that stage, most of the operational overhead is manual work that should be running on systems.
>
> We build the AI operating infrastructure that removes that layer — across every role from C-suite down. Not a tool your team adopts. A system we build and deploy.
>
> Worth a quick call this week?
>
> Vince

**Adjust line 1** to reference something specific from their recent LinkedIn activity or company news. One sentence of personalization doubles reply rate.
