## Product Definition
**AutoFlows** is a fully integrated AI dispatch system for businesses.

Used by every employee with a computer-based role — from C-suite to entry level.

Automates: clicking, thinking, and navigation grind.

Boosts: execution speed, quality, and scale.

## How the Product Actually Works
Employees get parallel AI chats running simultaneously — one per task they have for the day. Each chat does the research, clicking, navigation, and thinking the employee would otherwise do manually. The employee reviews the AI output quickly and uses it to complete the task. Fast review loop, not full delegation. They command, AI executes, human approves.
